# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 19, 2026

Stun Fun is an entertainment-only taser simulator app. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

## Summary

Stun Fun does **not** collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal information.

## Information We Collect

Stun Fun does not collect any personal data from users.

The app does not require account creation and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

## Camera and Flashlight Access

Stun Fun may request camera permission only so it can access the device flashlight/torch for the strobe effect.

- The app does **not** take photos.
- The app does **not** record video.
- The app does **not** analyze camera input.
- The app does **not** upload camera data anywhere.

The camera permission is used solely to turn the flashlight on and off while the simulator effect is active.

## Audio and Vibration

Stun Fun plays a bundled sound effect and may trigger device vibration. These features do not collect or transmit any data.

## Analytics and Tracking

Stun Fun does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or cross-app tracking.

## Data Sharing

Because Stun Fun does not collect personal data, it does not share personal data with third parties.

## Children’s Privacy

Stun Fun does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

## Data Retention

Stun Fun does not collect personal data, so there is no personal data to retain or delete.

## Security

Stun Fun is designed to operate locally on your device. No personal data is transmitted to our servers because the app does not use our servers.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this repository with an updated effective date.
